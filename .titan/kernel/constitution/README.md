# Titan Agent OS Constitution

The Constitution contains the highest-level engineering rules of Titan Agent OS. It is declarative and does not execute work.

## Article 1 — Human authority

Humans retain final authority over business goals, strategic architecture, destructive changes and production releases. Agent recommendations must remain transparent, evidence-backed and reviewable.

## Article 2 — Evidence before assertion

Current source, connected runtime behaviour and verification evidence outweigh branch descriptions, archive names, document titles and agent confidence. Unverified functionality must be labelled planned, source-present, partially wired, dormant or blocked rather than operational.

## Article 3 — One authority per responsibility

- MagicAI host owns authentication and platform membership lifecycle.
- WorkCore owns operational records, permissions, governed actions and mutations.
- Titan Zero owns intent, planning, orchestration and delegation.
- Interaction Engine owns interaction state, clarification, evidence, approval preparation and command preparation.
- Chatbot/PWA owns conversation, presentation, device storage and sync experience.
- Titan Vault owns credentials and protected configuration.

No component may create a parallel authority without an accepted architectural decision.

## Article 4 — Governed operational mutation

Every operational mutation must derive trusted tenant and actor context on the server and pass entitlement, permission, confirmation where required, idempotency, transaction, audit and domain-event controls. AI confidence never grants authority.

## Article 5 — Contracts before assumptions

Capabilities, actions, workflows, validators, policies, providers, agents, events and documentation sources use explicit versioned schemas. Hidden conventions and prompt-only rules are insufficient.

## Article 6 — Reuse before duplication

A capability, action, validator, provider, migration authority or operational model should have one canonical implementation. Compatibility copies remain disabled and documented until unique deltas are preserved and removal is verified.

## Article 7 — Privacy, tenancy and offline integrity

Company boundaries fail closed. Credentials never enter unprotected source, logs, prompts, queues or generated reports. Offline records, conflicts and unsynchronised data are never silently discarded.

## Article 8 — Planning and separation of planes

The Kernel defines rules. The Control Plane plans and governs. The Execution Plane performs approved work. Intelligence informs decisions. No control-plane component directly bypasses execution governance to modify operational business code.

## Article 9 — Documentation and memory

`/docs` is the canonical human project library. `/.titan/documentation` is the Agent OS documentation, status, progress, decision, review, learning, generated-view and Chronicle layer. Derived views identify their canonical sources and do not create duplicate manual truth.

## Article 10 — Controlled evolution

Lessons and observations may generate proposals, but Kernel, authority or release-policy changes require simulation, validation, relevant authority review and human approval where strategic or destructive. No autonomous evolution bypasses governance.
