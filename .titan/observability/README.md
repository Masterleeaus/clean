# Observability

Observability records the health and behaviour of Titan Agent OS and its engineering workflows.

## Planned areas

- metrics
- health
- performance
- audit
- activity
- alerts
- analytics

Metrics may inform trust, planning and Evolution proposals, but they do not silently grant permissions, change authority or approve releases.

Every metric and report identifies its source, collection method, window, confidence and freshness. Sensitive prompts, credentials, user data and unrestricted source content must not be emitted into observability records.

Automated telemetry collection is not yet claimed operational.
