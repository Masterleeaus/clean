# Decision Records

Decision records preserve why significant architectural and operating choices were made.

## Lifecycle

- `proposed/`
- `accepted/`
- `rejected/`
- `superseded/`
- `archived/`

Every decision records:

- ID, title, status and dates;
- context and problem;
- options considered;
- selected option and rationale;
- consequences and risks;
- related capabilities, policies and authorities;
- source evidence and affected paths;
- approval authority;
- supersession relationship when applicable.

Accepted decisions constrain plans and implementations. Rejected and superseded decisions remain queryable so agents do not repeat resolved debates.
